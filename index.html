Melodic Subtlety – Solo Piano

2006 · solo piano

Thirteen pieces, piano at the center. A whisper, a sojourn, a constellation, then Reine. Clarity is the one people already found.

Listen on Spotify

David Rivera is an American composer. He has made seven piano-led albums that blend minimalism and electronica. The music is piano at heart: subtle melodies for overlooked moments, sometimes with ethereal instruments, vocals, or synths, all played by David himself. More about David.

He is back at the instrument, and more music is coming. For now, start with these thirteen pieces.
